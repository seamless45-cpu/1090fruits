CONTROLS & SPECS

🎮 CROSS-PLATFORM CAMERA & ZOOM

• Max Range: 300 meters accurately calibrated in 3D space.

• PC / Laptop: Mouse wheel zoom, Trackpad pinch, or [PageUp]/[PageDown].

• Mobile / Tablet: Pinch-to-zoom multi-touch or use the on-screen zoom slider.

• Console / Gamepad: Right Stick Up/Down, Triggers L2/R2, or D-pad.

• TV: Dedicated on-screen zoom buttons [+] and [-] at the left.

• Camera Shake: High-frequency random positional translation ONLY on X, Y, Z. Rotational shake (pitch, yaw, roll) is 100% disabled for aiming comfort and clarity. Stronger when closer to explosions, weaker when farther.

⚡ SKILL CASTING & HOTKEYS

• Middle-Right Skill Bar: Compact panel showing exact skills matching equipped fruit or sword.

• PC Hotkeys: Z, X, C, V, B, F, G, N, M, L, K, J (only for active skills!).

• Mobile / Tablet: Use the dedicated [USE] button on the left of each skill row.

• Cooldown Fill: 100% to 0% animated wiping effect over the whole bar, with accurate second countdown timer.

⚔️ SEPARATED FRUIT & WEAPON INVENTORY

• Bottom horizontal square slots allow equipping/unequipping Fruit, Sword, and Gun independently.

• Press [TAB] or click the Active slot to switch between casting Fruit Skills and Sword Skills.

• Press [R] or click FIRE GUN to manually fire Rimefracture or Wildfire passive guns with auto-aim.
